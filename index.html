AFTER GRADUATE ADMISSION

Your plan of study is a separate milestone.

The supplied form states that students admitted with full pre-candidacy status should complete a plan of study no later than the semester of their 12th graduate credit. Work with your advisor, obtain departmental signatures, and submit it for graduate approval. Confirm the current process with ESU.

This is a graduate candidacy requirement, not an accelerated admission application.

GOOD QUESTIONS TO ASK

Before you take the next step.

Can I begin this master's degree while I am an undergraduate?

This guide has not verified an accelerated pathway for the Organizational Behavior concentration. Ask the coordinator to confirm availability, eligible undergraduate majors, entry requirements, and approval procedures for your situation.

Does the university's general accelerated policy guarantee eligibility?

No. The ESU accelerated pathways catalog describes baseline standards, including junior status (60 or more credits) and a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.00 before graduate coursework, with higher program thresholds possible. Those standards do not establish that this concentration has an approved pathway. Read ESU's accelerated pathways policy.

How many credits can I share, and how long will it take?

Obtain a program-specific answer from the coordinator. This guide does not promise shared credits, a five-year completion schedule, or a particular tuition rate for this concentration.

Is this an official application or eligibility checker?

No. This is an independent informational guide. The checklist helps you prepare; it does not evaluate or submit an application. Use ESU's official channels for admission decisions and current requirements.
